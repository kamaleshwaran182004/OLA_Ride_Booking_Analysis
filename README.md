 🚕 OLA Ride Booking Analysis

 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **100,000 OLA ride bookings** using **SQL and Power BI** to understand booking performance, cancellations, customer experience, vehicle performance, and revenue patterns. The objective is to identify operational trends, uncover business problems, and provide actionable insights to improve ride completion and overall business performance.



🎯 Business Problem Statement

OLA wants to better understand its booking and ride performance to improve operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and revenue. The management team is interested in identifying factors affecting successful rides, customer and driver cancellations, incomplete rides, vehicle performance, ratings, and booking value.

Business Question:

 How can OLA leverage booking data to identify operational trends, reduce cancellations, improve customer experience, and optimize overall ride and revenue performance?

 💡 Key Business Recommendations

* Reduce Cancellations – Identify major customer and driver cancellation reasons and take corrective actions.
* Improve Ride Completion – Address common incomplete-ride issues to increase successful bookings.
* Optimize Vehicle Performance – Focus on vehicle types with strong demand, ratings, and ride performance.
* Improve Customer Experience – Use customer ratings and waiting times to identify and improve service quality.
* Optimize Revenue – Focus on high-value rides, popular locations, and vehicle types to improve overall booking revenue.



 🎯 Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how **SQL and Power BI** can be used together to transform raw ride-booking data into meaningful business insights. The analysis helps identify cancellation patterns, operational challenges, customer experience issues, and revenue opportunities.
